What is ParkWise?

ParkWise is a smart parking reservation system developed specifically for Stevens Institute of Technology students. With ParkWise, you can view live availability in campus parking lots, reserve your parking spot ahead of time, and enter with a scannable QR code. Whether you're commuting or living on campus, ParkWise makes sure your parking is one less thing to stress about.

Key Features

Stevens Lot Integration

ParkWise tracks real-time space availability exclusively in Stevens Institute parking lots — not general Hoboken areas.

Student-Only Reservations

Only verified Stevens students can reserve a spot using their university login. Secure and fair access for all.

QR Code Entry

Each reservation comes with a unique QR code to scan at the parking lot gate, enabling fast and contactless access.

Web + Mobile Friendly

Reserve spots and check availability on both your phone and desktop browser – anytime, anywhere.

Currency Exchange Support

Coming soon: View parking rates in your preferred currency if you're an international student or visitor.

How It Works

1. Choose a Stevens Lot

Pick from available campus parking lots like Babbio Garage, River Lot, or Walker Gym based on real-time availability.

2. Reserve Your Spot

Select your time slot and confirm your reservation using your Stevens login credentials.

3. Scan and Park

Get a QR code for your reservation, scan it at the entrance gate, and park in your guaranteed spot.
