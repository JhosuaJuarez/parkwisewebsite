What is ParkWise?

ParkWise is a smart parking reservation system designed to solve campus parking struggles by providing real-time availability, secure reservation, and streamlined entry via QR codes.

Key Features

Live Availability

AI-powered camera systems monitor parking lot status in real-time to display open spots.

Reservation System

Reserve your parking spot ahead of time and avoid circling around in frustration.

QR Code Entry

Secure and fast lot entry using dynamically generated QR codes, scanned at the gate.

User-Friendly App

Simple web and mobile interfaces that allow users to view, book, and manage their reservations easily.

How It Works

1. Search

Find nearby available spots in real time using our smart availability map.

2. Reserve

Pick a time slot and reserve a spot instantly with just a few taps.

3. Park

Scan your QR code at the entrance and park worry-free in your reserved space.
